Terms & conditions

The rules of the road — in plain language where we can.

Last updated: January 2026. This is a sample. Replace with your lawyer-reviewed terms before going live.

1. Who this agreement is between

These terms apply between you (the renter) and mosrental (we / us). By booking a vehicle with us you agree to be bound by them.

2. Who can rent

  • You must be at least 21 years old.
  • You must hold a valid driver's license, unexpired at pickup and return.
  • You must present the credit or debit card the booking is tied to at pickup.

3. Booking & cancellation

Confirmed bookings may be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before the scheduled pickup. Cancellations inside the 24-hour window may be subject to a cancellation fee shown at booking.

4. Payment

The rental total is charged on pickup unless noted otherwise. A refundable security deposit is held on your card and released after a clean return. Add-ons and insurance are itemised and payable at pickup.

5. Fuel policy

Return the vehicle at the same fuel level recorded at pickup. If the fuel level is lower, we charge the current pump rate per litre of the missing fuel plus a refuelling service fee.

6. Mileage

Unless an unlimited-mileage package is explicitly included, each rental includes a daily mileage allowance shown on the booking. Additional kilometres are charged at the extra-km rate shown.

7. Damage

You are responsible for damage to the vehicle during the rental period, up to the deductible of the selected insurance plan. Pre-existing damage is photographed and documented at pickup. New damage is reviewed by a human before any charge.

8. Prohibited uses

  • No off-road use unless the vehicle is explicitly a 4WD rental.
  • No racing, driver training, or towing beyond rated capacity.
  • No smoking in the vehicle (cleaning fee applies).
  • No pets without written approval.

9. Late return

A grace period of 60 minutes is included free. Beyond that, each additional hour is charged at 1/8 of the daily rate until the full daily rate applies. Request extensions through My Booking.

10. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of your rental location. Disputes should be raised with us first; small-claims procedures apply thereafter.

If any clause here is unclear, write to our team — we're happy to explain.
