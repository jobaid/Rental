About mosrental

Modern car rental, built by people who hate paperwork as much as you do.

We started with a question.

"Why does renting a car take so long?" You wait at a counter. You sign four forms. You squint at terms you'll never read. You walk out an hour later.

We rebuilt the whole thing from scratch. Everything that can happen on your phone, does. Everything else — the keys, the eyes-on-license — happens at the counter in minutes.
